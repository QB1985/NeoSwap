**Manual level sync.**

- New **Level sync** option in each job's options (under Lockstyle): turn it on and pick a level, and that job's sets are worn as if you were that level - level tiers, level conditions and the gear picker's level filter all follow it. It never goes above your real level.
- `/nswap sync <level>` / `/nswap sync off` for your job now, or `/nswap sync <JOB> <level>` for another.
- The job's header shows *synced to ...*, and the bar at the bottom shows *[LvXX sync]* while it's on. The controller reaches it too.
