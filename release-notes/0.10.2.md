**Lockstyle changes take effect right away.**

- Switching a job's Lockstyle between **Off**, **Global** and **Own** now applies it straight away when you're on that job, instead of waiting for the next job change: Off sends `/lockstyle off`, Global and Own put that look on.
- Flicking through the choices (or cycling them with the controller) only sends the one you stop on.
- An *Own* lockstyle with no pieces yet just says so instead of sending anything.
