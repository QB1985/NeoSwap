**Totals include the gear underneath.**

- A set's totals, and the summary on its card, now count the pieces worn under it that it doesn't replace: your Idle set under Engaged, Resting, Chocobo riding, spells and items; Idle + Engaged under weapon skills, job abilities and ranged attacks.
- The totals title says what's included (*Totals with Idle + Engaged*).
- Only sets with no conditions (or just a matching *Status is*) count as underneath, in the order they'd go on.
