**Hints for what to put in a set, and text that fits.**

- Hover a choice in **Use it for** (or the hint beside it) for the bonuses that kind of set is good for: defense and damage taken - for Idle, haste, accuracy and Store TP for Engaged, Fast Cast for precast, hMP for Resting...
- Hover a **magic skill** or a **spell** for what helps it (INT and Magic Attack Bonus for elemental, Cure potency and MND for healing...).
- Hover a **weapon skill** for its stats (Tachi: Gekko: STR; Savage Blade: STR, MND...) and whether it's elemental or critical; a weapon skill set also lists the stats its weapon skills use. Many **job abilities** have hints too (Jump: VIT; Steal: "Steal" +...).
- Long text now wraps instead of being cut off (the *Use it for* hint, warnings, import notes, the checks), long tooltips wrap, and names cut short (sets, commands, gear, the bottom bar) show in full when hovered.

Weapon skill stats are the level-75 era values; CatsEyeXI's own changes may differ.
