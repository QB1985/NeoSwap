**Command buttons snap together.**

- Drag an on-screen command button near another and it snaps beside it, above or below it - lined up by the top, centre or bottom (or left, centre, right), with a small gap so they don't touch.
- While you drag, a dashed outline shows where it'll go and the other button's edge lights up; let go to put it there. Away from the others it goes wherever you drop it, as before.
- *Snap command buttons* in the settings (General) turns snapping off.
