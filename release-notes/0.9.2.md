**Controller:** the job's options are reachable now.

- Up from **+ New set** (or + New command) goes into the job's header: its **modes**, **Lockstyle** and **Macros**.
- Left / right changes a value (macro book and page, lockstyle Off / Global / Own); Confirm flips the Macros switch or a mode, and on an *Own* lockstyle opens its editor. The second action sets the macro book and page now.
- Down from the last option goes back to the list.
