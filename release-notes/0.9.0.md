First release of **NeoSwap**, a GUI gear swapper for Ashita v4 (built for CatsEyeXI) that replaces writing LuAshitacast profiles.

**Install:** download `NeoSwap-0.9.0.zip` below, unzip it into your Ashita `addons` folder (you should have `addons/NeoSwap/neoswap.lua`), then `/addon load neoswap`. Unload LuAshitacast first: two swappers will fight.

### What's in it
- **Set editor** shaped like the equipment menu, per job, with a stat summary per set, augments, and a gear picker you can search and sort by any stat.
- **When sets are worn:** Idle as the base, then Engaged / Resting / Chocobo riding, then actions (weapon skills, spells, abilities, items), with conditions (HP/MP, buffs, pet, weather and day, area, level, modes...) and priority.
- **Level tiers** inside a set for level-sync, with lower pieces carrying forward.
- **Checks** for conflicts, wrong slots / jobs, missing gear and pieces that cover a slot (cloaks).
- **LuAshitacast import** of your existing profiles, including level-milestone sets.
- **Your own commands** (cast, abilities, items, wait for pet, wear a set, switch / cycle modes and entries, stop unless...) with slash words and movable on-screen buttons (text or icon).
- **Custom entries** (HELM and the like) whose sets layer over any job.
- **Lockstyle** global or per job, **macro book / page** per job.
- **Controller** support, the same as NeoMOG.
- CatsEyeXI custom gear (Incursion armor, Thermal Ring) put on with /equip.
